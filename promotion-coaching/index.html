Process

How it works.

01

Decide where you're headed

We talk about where you want to be in a few years and what your next role needs to be to get there.

02

Pick what to work on

We compare your work to what your company expects at the next level, then choose one or two projects or skills that would help your promotion most.

03

Practice

Getting buy-in from people who don't report to you, handling conflict, presenting to executives. We practice, you try it at work, and we talk about how it went.

04

Ask for it

We write your promotion case together and plan the conversation with your manager.

FAQ

Common questions

Can you guarantee a promotion?

No. It also depends on your company's budget and timing. I can help you make a stronger case and get it in front of the right people.

Is this only for engineers?

No. I work with people in any tech role, including data, DevOps, product and program management, at any level.

How long does this take?

It depends on your company's promotion cycle. Four sessions can be weekly or every two weeks, and you can book more if you need them.

Does my manager need to be involved?

No, though it helps. They don't have to know you have a coach. If your company pays, we all agree on the goal at the start.

What if I'd move up faster somewhere else?

I'll tell you if I think so. A lot of what we put together will also work for your job search.
