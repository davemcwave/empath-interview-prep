Process

How it works.

01

Go over the feedback

We read the PIP or your last review together and work out what you're being asked to change. I'll tell you whether I think you can pass it.

02

Make it specific

If the feedback is vague, we turn it into a list of things to do. You take the list to your manager and ask them to agree to it.

03

Work on it

That could mean delivery, communication, or how things are going with your manager. We practice all the hard conversations.

04

See how it's going

We compare your work to the list. If it looks like you won't pass, we start your job search.

FAQ

Common questions

Can you guarantee I'll keep my job?

No. That's your company's decision. I can help you understand what they want, work on it, and start looking elsewhere early if it isn't going well.

Will my employer know I'm working with you?

Not from me. If you hire me yourself, it stays between us. If your company pays, you, your manager and I agree at the start on what I share. What you say in sessions stays private.

Can you help if I'm worried about layoffs?

Yes. I can't stop a layoff, but we can make sure people see your work, and get your resume and job search ready.

How long does this take?

PIPs usually run 30 to 90 days. Four weekly sessions cover a month, and you can book more if you need them.

What if I'm let go partway through?

You can use the sessions you have left for your job search, or I'll refund them.

Is this legal or HR advice?

No. I'm a coach. If you think you're being treated unlawfully, or you've been handed a severance agreement, talk to an employment lawyer before you sign anything.

What if I'd rather leave?

That's okay. We can work on your job search instead.
